# Privacy Policy for TailorPro

**Last updated:** March 2026

TailorPro ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the app.

## 1. Information We Collect

TailorPro uses authentication to allow users securely access their data.

When you create an account or log in, we may collect:

- Email address (used for authentication and account identification)

We do not collect or process sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary for authentication and app functionality.

## 2. How Your Data Is Used

Your information is used to:

- Create and manage your account
- Authenticate and identify you securely
- Associate your data (customers, orders, payments, etc.) with your account
- Provide core app functionality

## 3. Data Storage

Your data may be stored:

- Locally on your device, and/or
- Securely in your account via our backend service provider

We do not access or use your data for purposes outside of providing the app's functionality.

## 4. Third-Party Services

TailorPro uses Supabase as a backend service provider for:

- Authentication
- Secure data storage and processing

Supabase may process your data in accordance with their own privacy policy:
https://supabase.com/privacy

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not share your data except:

- When required to provide core app functionality through Supabase
- When you explicitly choose to share your data (e.g., exporting or sending files)

## 6. Backup and Export

TailorPro allows you to:

- Export your data as backup files
- Share files using third-party apps (e.g., WhatsApp, email, cloud storage)

These actions are fully controlled by you. We do not automatically transmit or share your data.

## 7. Internet Usage

Internet access is required for:

- Account authentication
- Syncing or retrieving account-related data

Some features may work offline depending on usage.

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, you are responsible for:

- Keeping your login credentials secure
- Protecting access to your device

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 9. Your Rights and Control

You can:

- Create and manage your account
- Access and manage your stored data within the app
- Export your data
- Delete app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage

## 10. Children's Privacy

TailorPro is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
